Code of Conduct
Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in the Archimet project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, experience level, identity, nationality, or personal characteristics.

We value open collaboration, thoughtful discussion, constructive feedback, and the sharing of knowledge. We want all contributors to feel safe asking questions, proposing ideas, reporting problems, and helping improve the project.

Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

Being respectful, considerate, and professional in all interactions.
Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully.
Criticizing ideas, implementations, and technical decisions without attacking individuals.
Respecting different perspectives, experiences, and approaches to problem-solving.
Providing clear, accurate, and useful information in issues, pull requests, discussions, and documentation.
Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from feedback.
Respecting the privacy and contributions of other community members.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, or deliberately hostile behavior.
Publishing private information without explicit permission.
Threats, stalking, or unwanted personal contact.
Deliberately disrupting discussions, reviews, or project collaboration.
Spam, malicious submissions, or intentionally misleading contributions.
Any other conduct that is inappropriate in a professional, collaborative open-source community.
Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:

GitHub issues, pull requests, and discussions.
Code reviews and contribution-related communications.
Project documentation and community channels.
Other spaces where someone is officially representing Archimet or its community.

The same expectations apply to maintainers, contributors, reviewers, and community members.

Project Maintainer Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards described in this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, issues, pull requests, or other contributions that violate these standards.

Maintainers will make reasonable efforts to review reports fairly, protect the privacy of those involved, and take appropriate action based on the circumstances.

Reporting Unacceptable Behavior

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please contact the project maintainer through the contact information listed in the repository's GitHub profile or README.

When reporting an incident, include relevant details, such as links to discussions or pull requests, if available.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Please avoid sharing sensitive personal information publicly when reporting an incident.

Enforcement

Depending on the severity and circumstances of a violation, maintainers may take one or more of the following actions:

A private warning requesting that the behavior stop.
Removal or editing of inappropriate content.
Temporary restrictions on participation in project discussions or contributions.
Temporary or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Serious or repeated violations may result in immediate restrictions without a prior warning.

Enforcement decisions will be proportionate to the situation and aimed at maintaining a safe, productive, and respectful community.

Appeals

Anyone subject to an enforcement decision may contact the project maintainer to request a review. Appeals will be considered fairly, taking into account the available information and the circumstances of the incident.

Our Commitment

Archimet is built through collaboration, curiosity, and continuous improvement. We encourage everyone to contribute thoughtfully, communicate respectfully, and help create an environment where good ideas can be explored and better software can be built together.

By participating in this project, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct.

This Code of Conduct is maintained by the Archimet project maintainers.
